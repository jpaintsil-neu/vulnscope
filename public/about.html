About VulnScope

Making cybersecurity vulnerability information easier to understand, organize, and act on.

Why VulnScope Exists

Security information can be difficult to interpret. VulnScope is designed to bring public vulnerability records, technology information, and personal review workflows into an approachable workspace.

Who It Is For

Everyday Technology Users

Understand security terminology and explore issues that may be relevant to the technology you use.

Cybersecurity Students

Explore real vulnerability records and connect cybersecurity concepts to practical examples.

Security Professionals

Review technical information and organize locally managed records, review statuses, and notes.

How VulnScope Works

  1. 1. Explore

    Search imported CVEs, apply filters, and open the source references.

  2. 2. Review

    Read record details and use Resources to understand the terminology and severity context.

  3. 3. Track

    Create local review records, maintain notes, and update their review status.

About the Data

VulnScope is designed around public data from the National Vulnerability Database (NVD), maintained by the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST).

The current vulnerability workspace uses an imported sample of NVD CVE records. Product integration is planned around the Common Platform Enumeration (CPE) Dictionary and its standardized technology identifiers.

Your workspace

Local review records

Your own vulnerability records, notes, and review statuses.

  • Create, read, edit, and delete your records.
  • Access is associated with the creating browser's workspace cookie.
  • Local changes leave the public NVD records unchanged.

Public source information is kept separate from locally managed records and review notes. Changes within VulnScope do not change records published by NVD.
